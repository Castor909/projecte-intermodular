# Intermodular Project – UD1B

## Consolidation of Project Development

---

### Unit Context and Objective

This unit corresponds to the second development phase of the project within the second trimester.

Having initiated development in UD1A, this phase requires a genuine consolidation of the technical work, demonstrating evolution, coherence, and continuity.

The objective is for the project to advance clearly, incorporate new functionality or complete what was started, and show improvement in structure, organisation, and quality.

---

### Tasks to Complete

- Complete at least one **core functionality** of the project, or develop **two distinct functionalities** with real integration.
- Demonstrate a **coherent application flow** and a clear improvement over UD1A.
- Improve **project organisation** (folder structure, separation of concerns, readable code).
- Make **descriptive and meaningful commits** to the repository.
- Keep **Trello up to date** and aligned with the actual state of the project.
- Expand the **technical documentation** with justified decisions, problems encountered, and solutions applied.

---

### Importance of the Presentation

In this phase, the following will be especially valued: the overall coherence of the project, an acceptable minimum visual quality, clarity in the demonstration, and the ability to explain what has been done.

> The project should start to look like a **product under construction**, not a technical proof of concept.

---

### Deliverables

| # | Deliverable |
